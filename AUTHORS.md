# Authors and acknowledgements

MacMateCaja is a macOS port and enhanced software fork of **MATE Caja 1.28.0**,
not a file manager written from scratch.

## Original MATE Caja and Nautilus

Thank you to the [MATE Desktop project](https://mate-desktop.org/), all
[Caja contributors](https://github.com/mate-desktop/caja/graphs/contributors),
translators and maintainers. The original Caja author list names Perberos,
Steve Zesch, Stefano Karapetsas and Jasmine Hassan, and credits the earlier
Nautilus teams. Caja continues their work, and this macOS port builds on it.

The complete original [AUTHORS](source/caja/AUTHORS), copyright notices in
individual files, and license files are retained. These include contributions
from the Nautilus developers, Red Hat, Eazel and many others; this short page
is not intended to replace that complete attribution.

- Original program: [MATE Caja](https://github.com/mate-desktop/caja).
- Original desktop environment: [MATE Desktop](https://mate-desktop.org/).
- Original license files: [COPYING](source/caja/COPYING),
  [COPYING.LIB](source/caja/COPYING.LIB),
  [COPYING.EXTENSIONS](source/caja/COPYING.EXTENSIONS).

## macOS port and enhancements

Adam Okoń maintains the MacMateCaja port and its macOS-specific enhancements.
This attribution covers the port work only, not authorship of upstream Caja.
The independent port is published at
[adamokon/MacMateCaja](https://github.com/adamokon/MacMateCaja).

## Other included software

We also thank the GVfs, dconf, mate-desktop, libegg and gvdb developers and
contributors. Their source trees retain their individual authorship and
licensing notices. GTK, GLib and the other runtime dependencies are likewise
essential to this port; their maintainers deserve credit.
